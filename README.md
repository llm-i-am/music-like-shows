# Concerts & Festivals — Tampa Bay & Orlando

A curated, frequently updated guide to upcoming live music around Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater and Orlando, with prices per person and direct links to tickets, details, or coverage for each show.

Shows are ranked in tiers: Tier 1 is the strongest recommendation, Tier 2 a strong pick, and Tier 3 a narrower pull. Shows flagged S-tier sit on the very top shelf. Past shows are listed on the site behind the Past toggle, so the season's history stays browsable after each date goes by.
