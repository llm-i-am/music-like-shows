About this guide

A personal selection of live music around Tampa Bay and Orlando, curated by Ben and kept up to date with help from Muse. It isn't a complete concert directory or a live ticket search.

Top pick, Strong pick, and Worth a look reflect the curator's taste. Curator favorites are separate from your own Favorites.

Shows are mostly selected around $100 per person per day or less, with intentional exceptions. All curated prices are shown unless you apply your own filter. Unknown prices are included by default.

Prices are listed per person, per show or per day as marked. Fees and ticket availability may change; confirm details with the event or seller before buying. Articles and event details aren't the same as ticket inventory. All event times are Eastern.

The current data does not provide a guide-wide verification time. An added date is when a show was found, not when its tickets were last checked.
